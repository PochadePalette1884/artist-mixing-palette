Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

We collect only what we need to run your account and membership, we never sell your personal information, and most of what you do in the palette stays on your own device. This policy explains what Pochade Palette LLC collects through www.artistsmixingpalette.com and why.

1. What we collect

What When Why
Email address and password You create an account To sign you in. Passwords are stored encrypted by our login provider; we cannot read them.
Date you accepted the Community Guidelines You create an account A record of the version you agreed to
Membership status, Stripe customer and subscription IDs You become a member To unlock member features and manage your subscription
Palettes you save and paintings you upload (members) You use those features while a member To show them on every device you sign in on
Basic technical data (IP address, browser type, pages viewed) You visit the site To deliver and secure the site and count visits

We do not collect your name, address or phone number unless you send them to us.

2. Payments

Payments are handled entirely by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never reach us. Stripe shares only your membership status, email and billing records with us. See Stripe's privacy policy.

3. What stays on your device

  • Browser storage. Your preferences, the palette you last used, palettes you build without a membership, your painting collection without a membership, and which free tries you have used are kept in your own browser. We do not receive them. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Paintings you add. Without a membership, paintings you load are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to us.
  • Camera (Live view). When you allow camera access, the picture is read in your browser to measure color. Video is never recorded, uploaded or stored.
  • Share images. Images made with the Share button are created on your device. They go only where you choose to send them.

4. Service providers

We use a few trusted providers to run the Service. They process data only to provide their service to us.

Provider What it does
Supabase Accounts, sign-in, membership records and members' saved palettes and paintings
Stripe Payments and subscription management
GitHub Pages Hosting the website
Plausible Analytics Privacy-friendly visit counts, without cookies or personal profiles
YouTube and Vimeo Playing AMP Studio videos; they may set their own cookies when a video plays
Google Fonts, jsDelivr Delivering fonts and code libraries the site uses

5. Email

We send account emails (confirmation, password reset) and essential notices about your membership or changes to our terms. We will not add you to a newsletter without your permission.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete your account data and members' saved palettes and paintings within 30 days. Billing records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to close your account. Write to pochadepalette@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

8. Children

The Service is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by access rules so each member can reach only their own records. No online service is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and let account holders know by email or on the site.

11. Contact
